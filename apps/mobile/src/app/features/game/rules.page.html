How scoring works

Pick five players you own a card of and name a captain. Every night, they score from their real box score. Your captain's points count double.

The lock

  • Your lineup locks at the first tip-off of the day, US Eastern time.
  • Changes made after the lock count from the next game day.
  • A player who does not play scores 0. There are no automatic substitutions.
  • A card added today counts from tomorrow.

Seasons and rankings

  • Weeks run Monday to Sunday. The season follows the regular season; preseason and playoff nights do not count.
  • Rankings show your username, never your email. You can leave the global ranking from your profile.
  • Private leagues count from the day you join.

Fair play

  • Free to play. No entry fee, no cash, no cards and no gift prizes: rewards are badges only.
  • Collections are self-declared. Card value, rarity and Premium never change a single point.
  • Premium adds leagues, past weeks and player details, nothing else.
