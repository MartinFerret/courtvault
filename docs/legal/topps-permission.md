# Topps image permission (summary)

Topps granted HoopTicker written permission to use their card images. The email itself is
confidential and is kept by Martin outside the repository; this note records only what the
site relies on.

- Scope used: the public website (hoopticker.com). Any use in the app, on social media or in
  ads needs Martin's confirmation against the original email first.
- Credit shown wherever an image appears: "Card images courtesy of Topps".
- Images come from official Topps material only, are stored and served from our own files
  (`apps/web/public/img`, originals in `data/images/topps/`, never hotlinked), and are shown only
  for the exact card, parallel or set they depict. Inserts and autographs outside the base
  checklist are shown in a labelled gallery, never as a catalog card.
- Recorded 2026-10-07.
